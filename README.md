#### For the connections, I used the user manual for the SpeedyBee F405 AIO V2, which can be found here (https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-f405-aio-v2-35-40a-bluejay-25-5x25-5-3-6s-flight-controller/) but is also included in the box.

#### In Components.pdf, you will find some explanations I put together regarding the components—based on my research—before I started assembling the drone. I did this to better understand what I was working with.

### What I could have done better with the drone:
- I could have chosen a larger carbon frame; the components are very cramped, and fitting them in was a real struggle. If I were to switch to a 5-inch frame, I might also need to replace the motors and propellers.
- The battery is likely too heavy for such a small drone; a 550–650 mAh battery would have been better for faster or improved flight performance.
- After ordering all the parts, I discovered a few Romanian websites selling drone components; some items were cheaper there than where I originally bought them (I’ll include links to these sites).

#### The drone hasn't been tested yet because the smoke stopper has only just arrived. I’ll post further modifications or updates after the first flight.
